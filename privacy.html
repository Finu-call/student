Privacy

StudentSave is designed so financial records are private to the authenticated account that created them.

Data

Your profile, transactions, budgets, savings goals and recurring payments are stored in Firebase Firestore under your user account.

Access

No transaction information is intentionally published publicly. Firestore security rules restrict reads and writes to the signed-in owner.

Deletion

You can permanently delete your account from Settings. StudentSave removes the user profile and stored finance records before deleting the Firebase Authentication account.

Authentication

Authentication is required to access personal financial data. Google sign-in and email/password are supported when enabled in your Firebase project.
